Cybersecurity case studies

These case studies were completed during the Google Cybersecurity Certificate. Each one is based on a simulated scenario provided by the course, and no real systems were analyzed. The topics include network traffic analysis, incident response, risk and vulnerability assessment, access control, regulatory compliance and basic system hardening.

Training

Google Cybersecurity Certificate

An entry-level online training program developed by Google and offered on Coursera, aimed at those who intend to enter the field of cybersecurity. It combines lessons, assessments and hands-on labs based on simulated scenarios.
